# Changelog

What changed in each version of Imouto Notes, newest first. The app shows the same list under Help.

## 0.8.0 (2026-11-02)

In All notes the cards are no longer cut off when there are many of them: their title and lines show in full, in the trash too.

## 0.7.6 (2026-11-01)

A new app icon: her face as she looks now, on a pink disc.

## 0.7.5 (2026-10-30)

Her settings in a window of their own, apart from the notes': from the tray, the settings, a note's menu or the 🐾 in All notes. All notes no longer shows the trash beside the list; Help says who made the app.

## 0.7.4 (2026-10-30)

Her Halloween costume: from 25 October to 1 November she wears a witch hat and greets you for the season. The hat is an item: under Look you can have her wear it always or never.

## 0.7.3 (2026-10-29)

Her face retouched: the short lines above her eyes are gone, and the strands over her left eye stay clean when she blinks, smiles or looks sad.

## 0.7.2 (2026-10-29)

The engine that animates her is updated to its latest version: she looks and moves just as before.

## 0.7.1 (2026-10-27)

A rolled-up note no longer grows taller at every start of the app.

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-27)

Pages as tabs in a note; a hub showing the note picked beside the list, with sorting, filters, dues and a trash; pictures, files, sound and backgrounds in notes; each note's own title and look, locking and a grid; a highlighter, text colours and coloured code; smart paste; export as text, Markdown and JSON; a backup a day; Windows notifications for dues; global shortcuts and a quick capture of the clipboard; the app in 14 languages.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-25)

Ready for more characters and skins: a character is picked in the settings once there is more than one, a skin is worn by season (Halloween, Christmas, Easter).

## 0.5.1 (2026-10-22)

The settings in a window of their own (from the hub's gear, a note's menu, the icon by the clock); buttons sized to their words; she goes on the first note opened; "Remove encryption" asks first; the notes and she are out of Alt+Tab.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-22)

Encrypted notes, one by one, each with a lock on its bar and its card: the password is the root (seven suggested words, or 20 characters or more) and the key travels in the notes folder, so another PC opens them with the same password.

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-21)

An audit's fixes, each with its check: no freeze when a note closes, no typing lost saving or reloading a change made elsewhere (the version replaced kept as a conflict copy); Quit waits for the notes to be saved; Done ticks the right box.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-21)

Tamburello's drawings, with a keyboard and a mouse in front of her and each paw pressing where it rests; a note's menu with "Imouto here", Tamburello and Show in File Explorer; a new note starts with a Markdown example in her words.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-21)

Spoilers in a note (||text||); nothing lost when Windows closes a note or the app quits; copies no longer take their note's id; much less memory for each extra note; Tamburello, her paws playing along with the keyboard and the mouse.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-19)

Sticky notes with a neko little sister who reminds you of due tasks: her figure, Obsidian-style writing, Windows voices, screenshot protection, any colour for a note.
